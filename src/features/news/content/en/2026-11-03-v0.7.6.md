0.7.6 gathers the recent updates: a stronger SXO-Harness assistant, richer annotation and database features, easier region comparison and ROI work, plus changes to downloads and sharing; backend compute was also optimized, so large datasets can keep running.

## SXO-Harness assistant

SXO-Harness is the chat panel opened from the navigation ball once signed in; it answers around your current dataset and page.

- Annotation is scored from multiple lines of evidence: mass accuracy, adduct fit and spatial distribution (blockiness and colocalization) are scored separately, with adjustable weights and a breakdown of each contribution.
- Chat accepts image attachments, and new bioinformatics tools cover HMDB mass lookup and KEGG pathway lookup (both take several queries per call), plus a pathway rollup over the current annotations.
- The assistant reads the current page's dataset and view state, so it can answer around the ion image, clustering or annotation you are looking at.

## Annotation and databases

- New [HMDB database](/database) page: keyword search, filters by origin and mass range, links out to HMDB entries, PubChem lookup and whole-table CSV download.
- Import full annotation libraries from the platform and reuse them locally.
- Annotation CSV now supports GBK / GB18030, so files exported from Chinese Excel import directly.

## Region comparison and ROI

- Import NPZ masks into region comparison and compare them with ROIs drawn on the canvas.
- Export differential and enriched ions from comparisons.
- ROIs highlight on the ion image and can be exported as masks.
- Gamma and display range are grayed out during multi-ion overlay where they don't apply.

## Downloads and sharing

- Downloads require a complete research profile (institution, position, field and region); fill it in on your [profile](/profile) page.
- Shared collection pages now require sign-in, and downloads use authenticated endpoints.

## Backend compute

- Backend compute was optimized for performance, so large datasets can keep running for long periods and long tasks are more stable.

## Docs and interface

- Unified guide structure, rewritten account pages and a new [database guide](/docs/guide/database).
- The homepage features section is bilingual, with auto-fitted gallery words.

## Do I need to do anything?

No, if your profile is already complete. If a download reports missing information, add it on your [profile](/profile) page.
