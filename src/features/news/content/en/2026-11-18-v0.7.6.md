## AI assistant

Open the assistant from the navigation ball after signing in. It runs as a floating window docked to the right of the page by default, leaving the workbench unobstructed. The assistant operates on the data you currently have open. Its main capabilities:

- **Context-aware answers.** The ion, clustering and annotations selected in the workbench are part of the assistant's context, so you can ask directly about what you are viewing — for example, the biological significance of an ion's spatial distribution.
- **Ion lookup and rendering.** Specify an m/z to retrieve the corresponding ion image; multi-ion overlays and mean spectra are supported, and the assistant can interpret spatial patterns that are difficult to explain.
- **Compound lookup.** HMDB, PubChem and ChEBI, searchable by name or mass, returning formula, exact mass, InChIKey and related information.
- **Pathway lookup.** KEGG mass search (adducts such as M+H and M+Na accounted for) and pathway queries, plus a rollup of the pathways behind the current annotations into a pathway list.
- **Literature search.** PubMed, Europe PMC and OpenAlex, returning titles, abstracts and DOIs, with general web search also available.
- **Image attachments.** Screenshots and figures from papers can be added directly to the conversation — for example, an ion image from a publication for the assistant to analyze.
- **Local session storage.** Past conversations are kept in the browser and can be resumed whenever needed.

## Backend EHPC

EHPC (elastic high-performance computing) is the analysis cluster behind the platform: once a dataset is uploaded, preprocessing such as denoising, baseline correction and peak detection, the imzML → Zarr format conversion, preview image generation, and UMAP dimensionality reduction all run on the cluster. The dataset size limit is raised from 20GB to 100GB (counting the imzML and ibd source files together). Datasets that previously exceeded the 20GB limit and could not be uploaded can now be uploaded in one go and sent straight to the cluster for processing.
