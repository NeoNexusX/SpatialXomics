# Navigation

Move around the SpatialXomics platform from the top navigation bar, or from the floating nav ball on full-screen pages.

**Author:** Chen Kejiang

**Feedback:** Found a bug or have a suggestion? Open a [GitHub Issue](https://github.com/NeoNexusX/MassVision/issues) or email **jydong@xmu.edu.cn**.

## Navigation Bar

The top navigation bar groups the platform into five sections:

| Section | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Home** | Landing page with the platform overview |
| **Datahub** | Public Datasets, My Datasets, and Collections |
| **Workspace** | New Analysis and the task dashboard |
| **Documentation** | Opens this documentation site |
| **Database** | Browses the built-in reference metabolite database (sign-in required, see [Database](./database)) |

![Navigation bar](https://cdn.neonexus.top/docs/20261021103908249.jpg_view)

Your avatar menu holds **Profile**, **Users** (administrators only), and **Sign out**. Signed-out visitors get **Sign in** and **Create account** instead.

## Floating Nav Ball

The Home, Sign in, and Create account pages, the dataset visualization page, and other full-screen pages hide the top navigation bar; a floating navigation ball appears in the bottom-right corner instead.

![Nav ball](https://cdn.neonexus.top/docs/20261021105000645.jpg_view)

Click the ball to open a side drawer. It mirrors the navigation bar except for two details: the drawer offers no sign-in/sign-up entry — signed-out visitors use the **Join to start** button on the home page — and sign-out is handled by the button on the nav ball itself, not in the drawer.

![Nav drawer](https://cdn.neonexus.top/docs/20261021105310451.jpg_view)
